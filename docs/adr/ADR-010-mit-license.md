# ADR-010: MIT License

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Interline is a personal productivity tool that handles meeting audio and transcripts locally. We want users, contributors and future customers to know exactly what they can do with the code, and we do not want a licensing discussion to slow down adoption.

## Decision

Interline is released under the MIT License.

## Consequences

- The source code can be inspected, modified and redistributed under the terms of `LICENSE`.
- The repository remains public and contains no proprietary, open-core or paid-tier code.
- All dependencies must remain compatible with MIT distribution.
